Quick answers

Before you start browsing.

The basics of how Meal Buzz works for diners and local food businesses.

Are the menus updated daily?

Meal Buzz is built around today’s menu. Kitchens can update dishes, prices and availability whenever they change.

Can I filter only Veg or Non-Veg food?

Yes. Use the Veg and Non-Veg filters to quickly narrow down nearby options.

Does Meal Buzz deliver food?

Meal Buzz currently focuses on discovery—helping you find the right nearby kitchen and today’s menu quickly.

How can a mess owner join?

Use the “For mess owners” option, register your kitchen and start publishing today’s menu.
